When a critical production line stops unexpectedly, the immediate instinct on the shop floor is to get it running as fast as possible. A technician resets a tripped drive, clears a jammed conveyor, or adjusts a sensor, and production resumes. But when the same breakdown returns two shifts later, it becomes clear that a "quick fix" isn't the same as fixing the problem.
